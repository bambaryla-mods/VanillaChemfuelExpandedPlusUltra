Each resource has its own network. A network is a set of pipes, plus anything connected to them: refineries, tanks, taps that hand the resource out as items, and drains that take items in.

- **Pipes** come in two kinds. The normal pipe is visible. The subterranean pipe is buried, so you can walk and build over it.
- **Valves** cut a network in two when closed.
- **Tanks** store the resource for the whole network.
- **Taps** let your colonists take the resource out as items, for example neutroamine for your doctors.
- **Drains** take items of the resource and add them to the network.

Everything sits in right-click dropdowns in the architect menu: one each for pipes, valves, tanks, taps, drains and refineries. The dropdowns also hold Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded's chemfuel and deepchem parts, and Helixien gas parts if you use Vanilla Helixien Gas Expanded.

The neutroamine network uses the same internal name as Vanilla Races Expanded - Android's, so the two mods share one network.
