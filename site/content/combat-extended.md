This page needs **Combat Extended**. Without it, none of this loads.

The FSX refinery makes FSX from chemfuel and neutroamine. With Vanilla Helixien Gas Expanded, it can also use helixien gas in place of chemfuel. The prometheum refinery makes prometheum from chemfuel and neutroamine.

FSX and prometheum tanks are **highly volatile**. A damaged tank explodes in flames, and the more it holds, the bigger the blast. Keep them away from anything you want to keep.
