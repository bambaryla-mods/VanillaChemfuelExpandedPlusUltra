## What the mod does

Vanilla Chemfuel Expanded lets you pump deepchem and pipe chemfuel around your base. This mod builds on the same pipe framework and gives you more to make with it:

- A **neutroamine refinery** that turns deepchem or chemfuel into neutroamine, with its own pipes, valves, tanks, taps and drains.
- A **raw plasteel refinery** and an **automatic weavery** that turn deepchem into raw plasteel and unrefined fibers. An autoclave or autoweave finishes them into plasteel, synthread, hyperweave or devilstrand, or your crafters can do it by hand at a smelter or tailoring bench.
- A **dye refinery** with a dye network, so you can paint your colony with dye you make yourself.
- With Biotech, a **toxic waste network** that carries wastepacks from pollution pumps, gestators and other waste makers to sealed tanks, a detoxifier, or the dye refinery.
- With Combat Extended, **FSX** and **prometheum** refineries and networks.
- A **Pipe limits** tab on refineries that pauses production when an input runs low or an output tank fills up.

Most products have three tiers of research, and each tier makes more of the product per day. Plasteel and the textiles have a fourth tier that makes them straight from deepchem or chemfuel and skips the intermediate step. All refineries, pipes, valves, tanks, taps and drains sit in right-click dropdowns, so the architect menu stays short.
